Decision Matrix决策矩阵

Comparing options? Weight what matters, score each option, mark the must-haves, and get a clear recommendation, plus a warning if the result is too close to call.

要在几个方案中做选择？给重要的标准加权、为每个方案打分、标出必须项，得到清晰的建议；如果结果太接近，也会提醒你。

  • For managers & engineers
  • 适合经理与工程师
  • Ready templates
  • 现成模板
  • Robustness check
  • 稳健性检查

2

Weights and scores权重与评分

Weight: how important (1–5). Score: how well each option does (1 poor – 5 excellent). Tick Must for deal-breakers.

权重：重要程度（1–5）。评分：每个方案的表现（1 很差 – 5 很好）。一票否决的标准请勾选必须。

3

Result结果

A matrix supports judgement; it doesn't replace it. If the winner feels wrong, ask which criterion is missing.

矩阵是辅助判断，不是替代判断。如果胜出方案感觉不对，问问自己漏掉了哪个标准。
