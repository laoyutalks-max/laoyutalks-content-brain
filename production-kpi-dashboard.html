How to use it如何使用

  1. Settings: enter your line name, month, planned minutes per day, ideal cycle time and targets.设置：填写产线名称、月份、每天计划时间、理想节拍与目标。
  2. Daily Log: each day, fill only the yellow cells: downtime, total units, good units and the main reason.每日记录：每天只填黄色格：停机时间、总产量、良品数和主要原因。
  3. Downtime Log (optional): log every stop for a more accurate Pareto.停机记录（可选）：记录每次停机，让 Pareto 更准确。
  4. Dashboard: OEE, availability, performance, quality, scrap, attainment and the downtime Pareto update by themselves.仪表板：OEE、稼动率、性能率、良率、报废率、达成率与停机 Pareto 会自动更新。
  5. The file comes with sample numbers in blue. Replace them with your own.文件自带蓝色的示例数据，请替换成你自己的。

What the numbers tell you这些数字告诉你什么

  • Availability high, output flat? The loss is in speed or minor stops. Look at Performance.稼动率高，产量却没变？损失在速度或小停机，要看性能率。
  • One reason is 40% of downtime? Fix that first. The Pareto shows where one action pays most.一个原因占停机 40%？先解决它。Pareto 告诉你哪个行动回报最大。
  • Quality dropping on certain days? Check the Notes column for shift, material lot or new staff.某些天良率下降？看备注栏：班次、物料批次或新员工。
