Make the weekly review work让每周回顾真正有效

  1. Review overdue items first. Ask what is blocking them, not who is late.先看逾期项目。问「卡在哪里」，而不是「谁拖了」。
  2. Every action needs one owner. “The team” is not an owner.每个行动只能有一个负责人。「团队」不是负责人。
  3. Close or cancel actions that no longer matter. A short list gets done.关闭或取消不再重要的行动。清单越短越能完成。
  4. Blocked for two weeks? Escalate it, with the cost of waiting.卡住两周了？带着「等待的代价」向上反映。
