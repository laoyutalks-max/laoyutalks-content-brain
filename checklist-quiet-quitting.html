Quiet Quitting Early-Warning Checklist员工默默躺平预警清单

Spot a team member who is quietly disengaging, check the system before you blame the person, have the right conversation, and follow up for 30 days.

及早发现正在默默失去动力的团队成员：先检查系统，再看个人；用对的方式谈一次话，并持续跟进 30 天。

  • For supervisors, team leads & managers
  • 适合主管、组长与经理
  • 4 parts · about 15 minutes
  • 4 个部分 · 约 15 分钟
  • Print or save as PDF
  • 可打印或另存为 PDF

1

Spot the signals识别信号

Tick what you have noticed in the last 4–6 weeks. Compare the person with their own normal, not with others.

勾选你在最近 4–6 周观察到的情况。要和这个人自己的平常表现比较，而不是和别人比。

0/10

This is a prompt to look closer, not a diagnosis. If you suspect personal, family or health issues, listen with care and point them to HR or your employee assistance programme rather than trying to solve it yourself.

这只是提醒你多关注，不是诊断。如果你怀疑是个人、家庭或健康原因，请用心倾听，并引导对方联系人事部门或员工援助计划，而不是自己去解决。

2

Check the system first先检查系统

Before you talk to the person, check what may have changed around them. Often the cause is in the system, not the employee.

在找对方谈之前，先看看他周围发生了什么变化。原因往往在系统，而不在员工本身。

3

Have the 1:1 conversation进行一对一谈话

Private, unhurried, and not about performance first. Use these questions and write down what you hear.

私下、不赶时间，开头不要谈绩效。用以下问题，并记下你听到的内容。

  1. “How are things going for you at work lately, honestly?”“最近工作上感觉怎么样？说实话就好。”
  2. “What slowed you down the most last month?”“上个月最拖慢你的是什么？”
  3. “If you could change one thing about how we work, what would it be?”“如果能改变我们工作方式中的一件事，你会改什么？”
  4. “Is there something you raised before that we never followed up on?”“你以前提过、但我们一直没有跟进的事情有吗？”
  5. “What would make the next three months better for you?”“怎样能让接下来三个月对你来说更好？”

Do要做

  • Listen more than you talk多听少说
  • Thank them for being honest感谢对方的坦诚
  • Agree one or two actions, in writing书面约定一到两个行动

Don’t不要做

  • Open with complaints about performance一开口就批评绩效
  • Promise what you cannot deliver承诺做不到的事
  • Discuss it in front of the team在团队面前讨论
4

Follow up for 30 days跟进 30 天

Trust comes back through visible follow-through. Re-do Part 1 at the end of week 4.

信任来自看得见的跟进。第 4 周结束时，重新做一次第 1 部分。

Week周Goal目标Action / owner行动 / 负责人Done完成
1Agree 1–2 actions in writing书面约定 1–2 个行动
2Deliver one visible action; close the loop on an old idea完成一个看得见的行动；回应一个旧想法
3Short check-in: what has changed for them?简短回访：对他来说有什么改变？
4Review together and re-score Part 1一起回顾，并重新评分第 1 部分
