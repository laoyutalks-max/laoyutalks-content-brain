01

Daily production report in 2 minutes2 分钟写好生产日报

Use it: Every morning, before the production meeting.使用时机：每天早上，生产会议之前。

Act as an assistant to a production supervisor.
Here is yesterday's raw data for [line / area]:
[paste output, target, scrap, downtime, manpower]

Write a daily production report with:
1. A 3-line summary against target
2. The top 3 issues and their likely causes
3. Actions for today, each with an owner
4. Anything my manager needs to know

Keep it under 200 words and in plain language. If data is missing, list what is missing instead of guessing.
你是一名生产主管的助理。
以下是 [产线 / 区域] 昨天的原始数据：
[粘贴产量、目标、报废、停机、人力]

请写一份生产日报，包括：
1. 与目标对比的 3 行总结
2. 前 3 个问题及可能原因
3. 今天的行动，每项注明负责人
4. 需要让我上级知道的事项

200 字以内，用简单直白的语言。如果数据缺失，请列出缺少什么，不要猜测。
02

Repeated mistake → root cause and controls重复错误 → 根本原因与控制措施

Use it: When retraining has not stopped the same mistake.使用时机：重复培训后同样的错误还在发生时。

The same mistake has happened [number] times in [period]:
[describe the mistake: where, which step, when it is found]
We have already repeated the training.

Act as a continuous-improvement engineer.
1. First ask me up to 5 questions you need answered.
2. Then give the likely root causes using 5 Whys.
3. Propose controls, strongest first: eliminate the step > error-proof (poka-yoke) > system check > visual aid > training.
4. Tell me how to check in 30 days whether the fix worked.
同样的错误在 [时间段] 内发生了 [次数] 次：
[描述错误：在哪里、哪个步骤、何时被发现]
我们已经重新培训过。

请以持续改善工程师的身份：
1. 先问我最多 5 个你需要知道的问题。
2. 然后用 5 Why 找出可能的根本原因。
3. 按强度从高到低提出控制措施：取消该步骤 > 防错（poka-yoke）> 系统检查 > 目视辅助 > 培训。
4. 告诉我 30 天后如何验证改善是否有效。

03

Manpower gap: who gets the hours?人力缺口：工时给谁？

Use it: When several departments ask for the same spare people.使用时机：多个部门同时要同一批机动人力时。

I have [number] hours of spare manpower today. Requests:
- [Department A]: [need, deadline, impact if not covered]
- [Department B]: [need, deadline, impact if not covered]

Act as an operations planner. Compare the options on customer impact, delivery risk, quality risk and cost.
Recommend how to split the hours, explain why, state the risk of what we will not cover, and draft what I should tell each department lead.
我今天有 [数量] 小时的机动人力。需求如下：
- [部门 A]：[需求、截止时间、不支援的影响]
- [部门 B]：[需求、截止时间、不支援的影响]

请以运营计划员的身份，从客户影响、交期风险、质量风险和成本比较各方案。
建议如何分配工时并说明理由，指出没有覆盖部分的风险，并草拟我要对各部门负责人说的话。
04

Customer audit prep: practise with a strict auditor客户审核准备：和严格的审核员演练

Use it: The week before a customer or ISO audit.使用时机：客户或 ISO 审核的前一周。

Act as a strict [customer / ISO 9001 / ISO 13485 / GMP] auditor visiting our [area] next week.
Audit scope: [paste scope, processes or SOP list]

Ask me 10 audit questions, one at a time, the way a real auditor would.
After each of my answers, tell me:
- whether it would pass,
- what evidence I should be ready to show,
- how to make the answer stronger.
你是一名严格的 [客户 / ISO 9001 / ISO 13485 / GMP] 审核员，下周要审核我们的 [区域]。
审核范围：[粘贴范围、流程或 SOP 清单]

像真实审核员一样，一次问我一个问题，共 10 个。
每次我回答后，请告诉我：
- 这样回答能否通过；
- 我应准备好出示哪些证据；
- 如何让回答更有说服力。
05

100 emails → what needs me today100 封邮件 → 今天哪些必须我处理

Use it: When your inbox is full and you need to decide fast.使用时机：邮箱爆满、需要快速决定优先顺序时。

Here are today's emails (sender, subject and first lines):
[paste]

I am a [your role]. Sort them into four groups:
1. Needs my decision today
2. Needs my reply this week
3. Delegate to [team member names]
4. For information only

For group 1, give one line on what is being asked and by when. Do not draft replies yet.
以下是今天的邮件（发件人、主题和前几行）：
[粘贴]

我是 [你的职位]。请把它们分成四类：
1. 今天必须由我决定
2. 本周内需要我回复
3. 交给 [团队成员姓名] 处理
4. 仅供参考

第 1 类每封用一句话说明对方要什么、截止何时。先不要起草回复。
06

Weekly KPI review → 3 specific actions每周 KPI 回顾 → 3 个具体行动

Use it: Before your weekly review meeting.使用时机：每周回顾会议之前。

Here are this week's KPIs against target:
[paste the table]

Act as an operations manager.
1. Which 2 KPIs need attention most, and why?
2. What questions should I ask the team before deciding anything?
3. Propose 3 specific actions, each with an owner and a date.

Refer to the actual numbers. No generic advice.
以下是本周 KPI 与目标的对比：
[粘贴表格]

请以运营经理的身份：
1. 哪 2 个 KPI 最需要关注？为什么？
2. 做决定之前，我应该先问团队哪些问题？
3. 提出 3 个具体行动，每个都有负责人和日期。

请引用实际数字，不要给空泛的建议。
07

Look past the dashboard: find the hidden problem看穿仪表板：找出隐藏的问题

Use it: When the dashboard looks fine but output does not improve.使用时机：仪表板看起来正常，但产出没有提升时。

My dashboard shows this for [line / area]:
[paste uptime, output, OEE, scrap, manpower, etc.]

Look past the averages.
1. What problem could be hiding here? Point out any mismatch (for example: high uptime but flat output).
2. What is missing from this dashboard that I should measure?
3. Give me 3 questions to ask on the shop floor today.
我的 [产线 / 区域] 仪表板显示：
[粘贴稼动率、产量、OEE、报废、人力等]

请看穿平均数：
1. 这里可能隐藏了什么问题？指出任何不一致（例如稼动率高但产量没变）。
2. 这个仪表板缺少了哪些我应该衡量的指标？
3. 给我 3 个今天要在现场问的问题。

08

Raw lab or test data → report draft原始实验 / 检测数据 → 报告初稿

Use it: When you spend hours turning results into a report.使用时机：需要花好几个小时把结果整理成报告时。

Here are the results from [test / experiment name]:
[paste the data table and any notes]

Organise this into a report draft with:
1. Purpose and method in 2–3 lines
2. Results summary with the key numbers
3. Anything out of specification or unusual, highlighted
4. Conclusion and recommended next step

Do not invent data. Mark anything you are unsure about as [CHECK].
以下是 [测试 / 实验名称] 的结果：
[粘贴数据表和备注]

请整理成报告初稿，包括：
1. 用 2–3 行说明目的与方法
2. 结果摘要与关键数字
3. 标出任何超出规格或异常的结果
4. 结论与建议的下一步

不要编造数据。不确定的地方标注为 [待确认]。

09

Let AI run a workflow, not just answer a question让 AI 管理整个流程，而不只是回答问题

Use it: When you want AI to plan a recurring job end to end.使用时机：想让 AI 端到端规划一项重复性工作时。

Help me run [recurring job, e.g. equipment maintenance planning] as a workflow.

Context: [equipment list, schedules, last failures, team size]

1. Map the workflow step by step: inputs, decisions, outputs.
2. Show which steps AI can do, which need a person, and which need approval.
3. Produce this week's plan as a table: task, owner, date, risk.
4. List what data I should keep collecting so next week's plan is better.
请帮我把 [重复性工作，例如设备保养计划] 当作一个流程来管理。

背景：[设备清单、计划、最近的故障、团队人数]

1. 逐步画出流程：输入、决策、输出。
2. 标出哪些步骤 AI 能做、哪些需要人做、哪些需要审批。
3. 用表格列出本周计划：任务、负责人、日期、风险。
4. 列出我应持续收集哪些数据，让下周的计划更好。

10

Check an AI answer before you trust it相信 AI 答案之前先验证

Use it: Before you act on any AI answer at work.使用时机：在工作中采用任何 AI 答案之前。

Here is an answer an AI gave me about [topic]:
[paste the answer]

Review it critically:
1. List every claim that could be wrong.
2. For each one, tell me how to check it at work: which data, SOP or person.
3. Rate how confident I should be in the answer overall, and why.
4. Tell me what information would make it more reliable.
以下是 AI 给我的关于 [主题] 的回答：
[粘贴回答]

请批判性地检查：
1. 列出每一个可能错误的说法。
2. 每一项告诉我在工作中如何验证：用哪些数据、哪份 SOP 或问谁。
3. 评估我对整个回答应有多大信心，并说明原因。
4. 告诉我还需要哪些信息才能让答案更可靠。
