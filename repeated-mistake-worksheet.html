Repeated Mistake Worksheet重复错误改善工作表

Retrained the team and it happened again? Find the real cause with 5 Whys, choose a control that does not rely on memory, and prove it worked in 30 days.

重新培训了，错误还是再发生？用 5 Why 找出真正原因，选择不依赖记忆的控制措施，并在 30 天内验证效果。

  • For supervisors, engineers & QA
  • 适合主管、工程师与质量人员
  • 4 steps · 20 minutes
  • 4 个步骤 · 20 分钟
  • Fill on screen or print
  • 可在线填写或打印

1

Describe the mistake描述错误

Already tried已经尝试过

If you ticked only these, the fix so far has relied on people remembering. That is why it keeps coming back.

如果你只勾了这些，目前的措施都依赖人去记住。这就是问题一再出现的原因。

2

Find the root cause: 5 Whys找出根本原因：5 Why

Ask “why” about the process, not the person. Stop when you reach something you can change in the system.

针对流程问「为什么」，而不是针对人。问到可以在系统中改变的地方就停下。

3

Choose controls: strongest first选择控制措施：由强到弱

Start at the top. Only move down when the stronger control is truly not possible.

从最上面开始。只有更强的措施确实不可行时，才往下选。

Control level控制层级Possible?可行？Action行动Owner / date负责人 / 日期
1. Eliminate取消Remove the step so the mistake cannot happen取消这个步骤，让错误不可能发生
2. Error-proof (poka-yoke)防错（poka-yoke）Make the wrong way physically impossible or detected at once让错误的做法在物理上不可能，或立即被发现
3. System check系统检查A scan, interlock or software check that stops the next step用扫码、联锁或系统检查阻止进入下一步
4. Visual aid目视辅助Colour codes, labels, photos at the point of use在使用点用颜色、标签或照片提示
5. Training & SOP培训与 SOPWeakest on its own. Use it together with a stronger control单独使用效果最弱，应与更强的措施一起使用
